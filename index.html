Mission 01Bachelor's thesis · 2024 · Solo

Spacecraft Descent & Landing Simulator

A simulator of the last minutes of a mission: fly a lander down to the surface of the Moon, Mars or a custom "Planet X" and set it down in one piece.

  • Flight modelGravity per body, wind gusts, engine thrust, and fuel burn that lightens the vehicle as it descends.
  • Entry sequenceHull temperature climbs on the way down; jettison the heat shield and deploy the parachute at the right moment or lose the vehicle.
  • Mission setupChoose lander and destination, then tune thrust, fuel load, gravity, wind and the touchdown velocity limit before each run.
  • Cloud-delivered assetsVehicle models and imagery are packaged as asset bundles and streamed at runtime from Microsoft Azure Blob Storage with private access keys, with a local-storage fallback.
  • C#
  • Unity
  • Azure Blob Storage
  • REST
  • Physics simulation
